# Translation guide (English -> Vietnamese)

Used for every episode so names, terms and pronouns stay consistent.
Keep lines short like subtitles. Keep Japanese honorifics the English subs keep
(-san, -dono, -sama, -ya, -chan) since the audio is Japanese.

## Pronouns
- Straw Hats among themselves: tôi/cậu (Nami, Robin, Usopp, Chopper, Brook, Franky, Zoro, Sanji). Luffy: tôi/cậu, "bọn tôi"/"chúng ta".
- Sanji to women: "Nami-san", "Robin-chan", self "anh"/"tôi".
- Anyone to enemies, thugs, angry outbursts: tao/mày, "bọn mày".
- Villains with presence (Doflamingo, Trébol, Diamante, admirals to pirates): ta/ngươi, "các ngươi".
- Doflamingo's family to him: "Doffy", "Thiếu chủ"; Doffy to them: ta/các ngươi.
- Samurai (Kin'emon, Kanjuro): tại hạ/các hạ; to Momonosuke: "cậu chủ". Momonosuke: ta/ngươi (proud kid).
- Law: tôi/cậu; calls Luffy "Mũ Rơm-ya".
- Fujitora: polite old man, tôi/cậu; "lão" when humble.
- Children / small characters: em/anh/chị as fits; Tontatta dwarves: chúng tôi/các người, very naive tone.
- Elders: ông/cháu; Riku (old king): ta/cháu.
- Rebecca: tôi/anh; to Kyros (Soldier): "ngài lính". Once he's revealed as her father: cha/con.
- Sabo <-> Luffy (sworn brothers): Sabo anh/em, Luffy em/anh. Same for Ace.
- Bartolomeo: worships Luffy, "Luffy-senpai", self tôi; rude to others: tao/mày.
- Cavendish: vain, ta/ngươi.
- Bellamy: tao/mày to enemies; with Luffy later cậu/tôi.
- Viola: tôi/cậu; Riku (old king) to Viola/Rebecca: ta/con.
- Usopp to the dwarves is "God Usopp" / "Usoland" -> "Thần Usopp" / "Usoland".
- Leo and Tontatta dwarves to Usopp: "ngài Usoland"; among themselves: chúng ta.
